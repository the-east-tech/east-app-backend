Privacy Policy

How Flow handles the information needed to run everyday business operations, protect accounts and support the people using the app.

In brief. Flow is a business operations application. It processes staff and workplace information to provide features such as attendance, stock, tasks and reporting. It is not an advertising platform and does not sell personal information.

1. Scope and responsibility

This Privacy Policy applies to the Flow mobile application and its related services. Flow is operated by Sequosal. Businesses that use Flow may create and administer staff accounts and determine which workplace records are entered. Their authorised administrators may have access to information relating to their business and employees.

For questions about this policy or how Flow processes information, contact admin@sequosal.com.

2. Information we collect and process

  • Account and staff details: names, business affiliation, employee IDs, roles, telephone numbers, profile photographs and, where provided by an authorised administrator, dates such as birth, joining or leaving dates.
  • Attendance: check-in and check-out records, QR-related attendance events, timestamps, location coordinates, location-derived addresses and distance from a configured workplace when attendance features are used.
  • Workplace content: stock and receiving records, images supplied for stock checks or reports, tasks, SOP-related information, sales and waste reports, complaints, approvals, points and other user-entered business records.
  • Device and technical information: app version, device platform, push-notification registration tokens, network request metadata such as IP addresses, authentication/session information and technical diagnostics or error details.
  • Support information: messages and diagnostic reports submitted for support, together with related account or business context where needed to investigate an issue.

Some business information may contain personal data entered by users. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in free-text fields or photographs.

3. Device permissions

  • Location: used when submitting attendance check-in or check-out evidence and validating the workplace location. Flow does not intentionally track location continuously in the background.
  • Camera and photographs: used for QR scanning and when a user takes or selects images for features such as stock and business reporting. Flow does not collect facial biometric templates for attendance.
  • Contacts: when a user chooses the contact picker, Flow can read contact names and telephone numbers on the device so the user can select a number for a staff record. The full address book is not uploaded as part of this picker; a selected telephone number can be submitted if the user proceeds to save it.
  • Notifications: used to provide operational notifications if the user allows them. Push delivery involves a device registration token.

Permission availability depends on the device and operating system. Some app functions may not work without the permissions they require.

4. How information is used

We process information to authenticate accounts, manage business permissions and staff, record attendance, maintain operational and audit records, display workplace reports, send requested notifications, provide support, investigate errors, secure the service and maintain its reliability.

Where a user explicitly requests translation of business text, the relevant text may be processed by a translation service. Normal viewing of already translated content does not require a new translation request.

Flow does not use personal information for third-party advertising or sell personal information.

5. Who may receive information

Information may be accessible to authorised users of the relevant business, subject to their assigned roles. We may also use providers to operate specific services, including:

  • Railway and database infrastructure: hosting, application processing and storage.
  • Cloudflare: network delivery and, when explicitly requested, text translation through Workers AI.
  • Google services: Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications and Google Places for business-location features.
  • Resend: delivering support and technical error-report emails when configured.

These services process information relevant to the functionality being provided and may operate in different countries. We may disclose information where required by applicable law or necessary to protect users, the service or legal rights.

6. Security

Flow uses HTTPS for data in transit, authentication and role-based access controls, password hashing and device security mechanisms for locally stored session credentials. We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, although no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention and deletion

Business and staff information is generally retained while required to operate the relevant business account, maintain legitimate operational records, meet legal obligations or resolve security and support issues. Retention can vary by the type of record. Technical logs and backups may be retained for limited operational periods before expiry or removal.

Staff accounts are generally managed by the relevant business administrator. An authorised administrator may delete eligible user records through Flow's management functions, subject to dependencies and access controls. Removing an account does not necessarily remove independent business records such as historical attendance, reports or audit information that must be retained for legitimate purposes.

To request access, correction or deletion, contact your business administrator or email admin@sequosal.com. Include enough information for us to identify the relevant account or business, but do not email passwords. We will review requests and explain any records that cannot immediately be removed because of legal, security or legitimate business requirements.

8. Your choices and requests

Depending on applicable law and your relationship with the organisation that manages your account, you may request information about your personal data, corrections or deletion. You can also manage device permissions in your operating-system settings. Revoking permissions may limit related features.

Flow is used with business-managed accounts rather than open public self-registration. If your account was provided by your employer or another organisation, that organisation may be the first point of contact for employment-related records.

9. Children

Flow is intended for business operations, not for children. It is not designed or marketed as a service for children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Flow's functionality or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published here with an updated date.
